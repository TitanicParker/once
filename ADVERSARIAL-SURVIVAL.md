# Adversarial Survival

## Strongest benign model

This was a genuinely mixed case. Structural foot pathology was real and repeatedly treated. The patient himself initially distinguished foot pain from leg tension and had mechanical/load-related improvement. Young-onset Parkinsonism was severe, but treatment produced real ocular, behavioural, choreiform and dyskinetic toxicity. Clinicians therefore adapted cautiously. Neurological foot phenomena were recognised over time; OFF states were treated; pregabalin helped pain; CR was used once a clear overnight pattern emerged. A dual model in which structural/neuropathic pain is worsened by OFF Parkinson’s is clinically coherent. No single mechanism explains the whole phenotype.

## Strongest path-dependent model

The July 2017 causal uncertainty was genuine, but the practical solution divided the problem into structural/Podiatry and neurological treatment pathways before the relationship was resolved. Later evidence repeatedly crossed that boundary: procyclidine foot/gait effects, GP re-linking of feet to tone/gait, Parkinsonian foot throw, OFF dystonia/undertreatment, Stalevo pain/function effects, medication-timed morning symptoms and CR relief. The record shows repeated local adaptation but no comparably explicit return to the original “Not clear” question. The explanatory architecture could accommodate each new event as structural pain, neuropathic pain or Parkinsonian modulation without a visible criterion for when the global model itself must change.

## What survives both models

1. Structural foot pathology was genuine.
2. Neurological lower-limb pathology was genuine.
3. Their relationship was expressly unresolved in 2017.
4. April 2017 bilateral pain cannot be retro-localised to L5.
5. Recurrent left fifth-MTPJ pathology was genuine and is the dominant persistent focal signal.
6. Some foot/lower-limb phenomena were neurologically treatment-state sensitive.
7. Response does not prove generator.
8. Pain and posture were partly dissociable.
9. Pregabalin response does not prove one neuropathic mechanism.
10. Sinemet response does not prove one Parkinsonian pain generator.
11. Treatment caution had real patient-specific support.
12. Management changed repeatedly.
13. No explicit comprehensive historical re-synthesis is identified.
14. Clinical significance of that absence is expert-dependent.
15. 2026 PRKN confirms the genetic branch but cannot be backdated.

## Claims that fail adversarial testing

- All foot pain was Parkinsonian.
- Podiatry was wrong or inappropriate.
- The corn was caused by dystonia.
- Procyclidine proved the corn was neurological.
- Later burning was the old fifth-MTPJ pain.
- Sinemet/CR response proves mechanism.
- CR necessarily should have been used years earlier.
- Management never changed.
- 2017 deferral was obviously wrong.

## Strongest surviving case

The durable issue is **reassessment governance**: whether an initially reasonable unresolved mixed formulation was sufficiently answerable to later cross-domain evidence, given a management system that visibly updated individual treatments more often than it explicitly reconstructed the original explanatory model.
