# 30 Core Expert Questions

These questions are the control grid for the repository. Each is framed so an expert can answer either way.

## A. Original mixed presentation

### EQ-01 — Structural disease versus neurological loading
Given severe Parkinsonism, dystonic-looking lower-limb posture/gait, pes planus, hammertoes, corns/calluses and pain in Jul 2017, what relationship between neurological syndrome and structural foot pathology should reasonably have been considered? Could tone/posture/clawing/gait alter loading without proving they caused the lesions?

### EQ-02 — Meaning of “Not clear”
Was Neurology’s “Not clear” formulation reasonable provisionally? What later events would ordinarily require it to be revisited?

### EQ-03 — GP versus Neurology formulation
The GP placed gait, feet, tone, clawing, initiation and function within one neurological presentation; Neurology later split local foot care while leaving causation unresolved. Are these contradictory formulations or legitimate differences in level of analysis?

### EQ-04 — Patient’s early distinction
What weight should be given to the patient’s early distinction between leg tension and foot pain, including improvement after reduced standing? Does this support a mechanical component without excluding neurological modulation?

## B. Podiatry and ownership

### EQ-05 — What Podiatry could answer
What questions could routine Podiatry reasonably answer, and what questions about Parkinsonian/dystonic loading would ordinarily remain with Neurology?

### EQ-06 — Was an integration loop required?
Would reasonable care in a mixed structural-neurological presentation require an explicit feedback/integration mechanism between Podiatry and Neurology, or could parallel care reasonably proceed without one?

### EQ-07 — Recurrent fifth-MTPJ lesion
What is the clinical significance of repeated left fifth-MTPJ pathology and the Dec 2018 convergence of focal pain, objective callus and targeted offloading? Does recurrence raise a biomechanical loading question without proving its source?

### EQ-08 — Podiatry endpoint
Given that the final Mar 2019 plan anticipated further care but the record does not explain why the pathway ceased, what significance can responsibly be attached to that discontinuity?

## C. Healy, genetics and treatment strategy

### EQ-09 — Galway versus Healy on levodopa
How should Healy’s low-dose dopamine-replacement model for probable PARK2 be compared with Galway’s desire to hold levodopa back insofar as possible? Compatible, materially different, or indeterminate?

### EQ-10 — “Unless you’d advise otherwise?”
Once Galway explicitly invited Healy to reconsider its deferral policy, what would constitute adequate documented consideration of his response?

### EQ-11 — PARK2 and therapeutic expectations
Should probable PARK2 have altered expected levodopa responsiveness/dyskinesia balance before genetic confirmation, and how much could reasonably be acted upon?

### EQ-12 — Genetics versus management neutrality
Was it reasonable to regard genetic testing as unlikely to change management while PARK2 was thought relevant to prognosis and treatment sensitivity? When should that assumption be revisited?

## D. Procyclidine natural experiment

### EQ-13 — What does withdrawal establish?
What can properly be inferred from major improvement on procyclidine and deterioration involving feet/gait/tension during withdrawal? Only treatment-state sensitivity, or more?

### EQ-14 — Outside-left-foot phenomenon
How should rapid change in the outside-left-foot phenomenon during neurological drug withdrawal be reconciled with genuine recurrent fifth-MTPJ structural pathology?

### EQ-15 — Reassessment threshold after procyclidine
Should neurological treatment-state effects involving feet/gait have triggered explicit reconsideration of the original “Not clear” relationship? If so, what assessment?

## E. Levodopa-deferral frame

### EQ-16 — Original caution
Was delaying levodopa/dopamine agonists in 2017 reasonable movement-disorders practice given exceptionally young onset and anticipated dyskinesia/ICD risk?

### EQ-17 — Later validation of risk
How should later chorea, ICD concerns and levodopa dyskinesia affect retrospective assessment of the original caution? Do they validate caution generally without determining its optimal duration?

### EQ-18 — “As long as humanly possible”
By 2019, was continued strong levodopa restraint still reasonable, and what developments should have caused reconsideration?

## F. 2020 neurological foot evidence

### EQ-19 — Parkinsonian foot throw
What is the significance of the 2020 classification of the foot-throw movement as “actually parkinsonian”? Does it change the broader historical foot interpretation or only identify one motor component?

### EQ-20 — Sensitivity/excess movement
How should exquisite foot sensitivity and excessive/restless movement be classified in a young Parkinsonian patient, and should these findings have prompted reassessment of earlier peripheral/mechanical explanations?

## G. 2021 Sinemet transition

### EQ-21 — What should have been measured?
Given prior treatment-state sensitivity, what lower-limb outcomes should reasonably have been reassessed when procyclidine was removed and Sinemet introduced: onset, wearing-off, overnight state, morning steps, focal left pain, clawing/curling, gait/loading or others?

### EQ-22 — IR Sinemet versus measurement failure
Does the record support criticism of immediate-release Sinemet itself, or is the stronger issue that the regimen is not shown to have been prospectively evaluated against the foot phenotype?

### EQ-23 — Continuity of the old plan
When Sinemet began but Neurology continued to stress minimal dosing and sparing, did this represent a genuinely new strategy or a modification of the original risk architecture?

## H. 2022–2024 competing explanations

### EQ-24 — Morning warm-up
How should 20–25 minute morning foot “warm-up” and soreness be investigated in a levodopa-treated patient? Mechanical warming, medication onset, OFF state, pain adaptation or mixture?

### EQ-25 — OFF/undertreatment versus neuropathic pain
How should clinicians reconcile severe bilateral burning and pregabalin benefit, the patient’s ten-day no-difference report, and separate evidence of morning/later shuffling, leg pain and undertreatment/OFF state?

### EQ-26 — Pain versus curling
What does substantial Lyrica pain relief with persistent toe curling imply? Should pain, dystonia/posture and gait have been monitored as separate outcomes?

## I. Pregabalin/Lyrica provenance and rationale

### EQ-27 — Who introduced Lyrica, and why?
The contemporaneous record says pregabalin was commenced by the GP for severe foot/leg pain; the 2025 response says it was introduced partly to spare further Sinemet. Does the record support the latter as original prescribing rationale or only as later Neurology rationale?

### EQ-28 — Later balancing versus original purpose
By 2025 Neurology explicitly considered increasing Lyrica to avoid more Sinemet because of dyskinesia risk. Is it legitimate to retrospectively describe Lyrica as part of a Sinemet-sparing strategy if that purpose arose only after GP initiation?

## J. 2025–2026 integration threshold

### EQ-29 — CR and overnight hypothesis
What can legitimately be inferred from severe morning feet, first 150 steps “torture,” relief after Parkinson medication, the overnight gap, targeted bedtime CR and later reported “profound relief”? Does this establish a treatable medication-state component without establishing the primary pain generator?

### EQ-30 — Global reassessment threshold
Considering 2017 “Not clear,” structural recurrence, procyclidine state effects, probable PARK2, Parkinsonian foot throw, 2021 levodopa introduction, 2022 morning symptoms, 2023 OFF/undertreatment, pregabalin-responsive pain, persistent curling, 2025 medication-timed relief and 2026 PRKN-confirmed Parkinsonism/dystonia: **at what point, if any, would reasonable movement-disorders practice have required an explicit integrated reassessment of the original structural/peripheral/neurological partition rather than continued component-specific treatment?**
