# Expert Questions

These are the five questions generated by the organising essay, [`clinical-argument-readable-updated-1.html`](clinical-argument-readable-updated-1.html). They are intentionally open questions. The expert is not asked to accept the essay's conclusion in advance.

## 1 — Original 2017 formulation

**Was “Not clear” a reasonable provisional formulation in July 2017, and what later evidence would ordinarily require that provisional relationship to be expressly reconsidered?**

## 2 — Management architecture

**Was the architecture clinically coherent: 2017–2021 deliberate deferral plus parallel structural foot care, followed after 2021 by constrained/minimised levodopa exposure while the inherited mixed pain model remained available?**

## 3 — Longitudinal treatment-state evidence

**Taken together, how strongly did the 2017–2025 sequence establish that important parts of the foot/lower-limb domain were neurological-treatment-state sensitive while other pain components remained structural, neuropathic or otherwise separable?**

## 4 — Re-evaluation versus local adaptation

**Does the longitudinal record show adequate re-evaluation of the original foot–neurology relationship, or principally repeated local management within an inherited mixed model whose underlying classification remained substantially intact?**

## 5 — Ultimate threshold question

**At what point, if any, did the accumulated evidence make continued management within the inherited mixed structural/peripheral/neurological model insufficient without an explicit integrated re-evaluation?**

If the expert considers that such a threshold existed, the expert should identify the approximate point and explain what a reasonable reassessment would ordinarily have included. If no such threshold existed, the expert should explain why the documented management and reassessment were sufficient.

---

## Evidential controls

- Documentary absence is not proof that a clinician never considered or discussed a matter.
- Treatment response establishes modifiability, not the primary mechanism of every symptom.
- Genuine structural fifth-MTPJ pathology and other non-neurological pain evidence must be considered alongside neurological-treatment-state evidence.
- The February 2026 PRKN confirmation may validate the earlier PARK2 diagnostic branch but must not be back-projected as knowledge available to clinicians in 2017–2025.
