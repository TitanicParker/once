# Documentary Fact Register

## Status and rule of use

This file is the **fact layer** of the clinical repository.

It is intentionally inclusive. At this stage no fact is excluded because it appears weak, adverse, duplicative, peripheral, inconsistent with another source, or unhelpful to a later proposition. The purpose is to preserve the factual field before tensions, propositions or expert questions are derived.

The controlling evidential source remains [`complete-record.md`](complete-record.md). `CLIN-` locators below point back to that record.

### Inclusion rule

- A **documentary fact** is something the source records as having been said, observed, prescribed, done, reported, measured, planned, stopped, restarted, diagnosed, queried, or communicated.
- A fact may be a clinician observation, patient report, test result, prescription, treatment response, administrative act, or documentary state.
- A recorded opinion is included as the fact that the opinion was recorded; it is not silently converted into objective truth.
- Patient recollection is retained as patient recollection.
- Later retrospective clinician explanation is retained as retrospective explanation.
- Documentary absence is not converted into proof that an event did not occur.
- Treatment response is recorded without assuming mechanism.
- Conflicting facts are preserved side-by-side and are not reconciled here.
- Exact duplicated source entries may be represented once in prose, but the duplicate source locators remain available in `complete-record.md`.

### Lossless control

This register is a normalised working layer, not a substitute for the evidence record. **Every factual statement contained in `complete-record.md` remains incorporated into the factual field even where it has not yet been reduced to a separate numbered sentence here.** Nothing in this file may be used to exclude a source fact merely because it has not yet been individually normalised.
