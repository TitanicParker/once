# Propositions

Status: **DOCUMENTARY**, **STRONG INFERENCE**, **EXPERT-DEPENDENT**, **CONTESTED**, **ABANDONED**.

## Surviving propositions

### P-01 — Mixed structural–neurological presentation
**DOCUMENTARY.** By Jun–Jul 2017 genuine structural foot pathology and genuine severe neurological lower-limb pathology coexisted. Their relationship was expressly unresolved.

### P-02 — Podiatry was legitimate but non-investigative as to causation
**STRONG INFERENCE.** Podiatry had a genuine structural treatment object. The surviving referral wording allocated local care but did not define the neurological–biomechanical causal question for Podiatry to resolve.

### P-03 — Recurrent left fifth-MTPJ/outside-left region is the dominant persistent focal signal
**DOCUMENTARY / STRONG INFERENCE.** Repeated objective fifth-MTPJ pathology and Dec 2018 pain-site convergence support focal continuity. This does not make every later foot pain the same lesion.

### P-04 — Foot/lower-limb phenotype was neurologically treatment-state sensitive before levodopa
**DOCUMENTARY.** Procyclidine treatment/withdrawal materially altered the physical state and deterioration included feet/gait/tension. Mechanism remains unresolved.

### P-05 — Initial levodopa caution had substantial patient-specific support
**DOCUMENTARY + EXPERT-DEPENDENT.** Very young onset, later chorea/ICD effects and levodopa dyskinesia provide real support for caution. Whether the duration/intensity of restraint was optimal requires expert opinion.

### P-06 — Healy advice did not simply ratify “hold back as long as possible”
**STRONG INFERENCE.** Healy supported probable PARK2, low-dose dopamine replacement and over-use caution. He did not order immediate levodopa, but his formulation was not textually identical to Galway’s more prolonged deferral policy.

### P-07 — Local updating is more visible than global explanatory updating
**STRONG INFERENCE.** The record contains many treatment changes but no comparably explicit historical reconstruction of the original “Not clear” structural–neurological relationship.

### P-08 — 2021 is a treatment-design/measurement question, not simply a medication-start event
**EXPERT-DEPENDENT.** Given prior treatment-state sensitivity, the absence of a documented structured foot-by-dose assessment raises whether the new regimen was adequately evaluated against an already-known lower-limb signal. It does not prove IR Sinemet was wrong.

### P-09 — 2022–2025 chronology is consistent with a clinically relevant coverage component
**EXPERT-DEPENDENT.** Morning warm-up, 2023 undertreatment/OFF evidence and 2025 overnight/first-step response are consistent with medication-state coverage being one component. They do not prove that the same untreated state existed unchanged since 2021.

### P-10 — Pain and posture are multidimensional
**DOCUMENTARY.** Pregabalin relieved substantial pain while toe curling persisted; focal mechanical pain, broad burning pain and OFF/motor states must be assessed separately.

### P-11 — Pregabalin provenance is not the same as later Sinemet-sparing use
**DOCUMENTARY / STRONG INFERENCE.** GP initiation for severe pain is contemporaneously documented. Neurology later genuinely used Lyrica as an alternative lever to further Sinemet escalation. The 2025 claim that it was introduced partly to spare Sinemet is a retrospective purpose attribution not established at initiation.

### P-12 — Complaint response is partly a retrospective defence of the original policy
**DOCUMENTARY / STRONG INFERENCE.** The response expressly quotes and relies upon the 2017 levodopa-sparing rationale, while explaining later Sinemet and Lyrica management as continuous with it.

### P-13 — The old plan remained functionally alive at a higher level after levodopa began
**STRONG INFERENCE.** Literal levodopa non-use ended in 2021; the governing principles of dopamine minimisation and separability of primary pain from Parkinsonian modulation remained recognisable through 2025.

### P-14 — The key question is reassessment threshold, not initial error
**EXPERT-DEPENDENT.** The strongest clinical question is whether and when accumulating cross-domain evidence required explicit integrated reassessment of an originally reasonable unresolved formulation.

### P-15 — PRKN confirmation validates the early genetic branch but not the foot mechanism
**DOCUMENTARY.** 2026 confirms Parkin-related Parkinsonism/dystonia and makes the early PARK2 line materially important; it cannot prove the corn, burning pain or treatment standard retrospectively.
