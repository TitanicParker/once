# Clinical Documentary Case

This repository is deliberately flat and clinical-only. The **clinical argument essay is the organiser and front door**. The other files exist to operationalise, sequence, and verify that argument.

## Reading order

1. **[clinical-argument-readable-updated-1.html](clinical-argument-readable-updated-1.html)** — the organising essay. It states the five expert propositions, the evidential controls, the counterevidence, the competing benign/path-dependent interpretations, and the strongest surviving formulation.
2. **[EXPERT-QUESTIONS.md](EXPERT-QUESTIONS.md)** — the five questions generated by the essay. These are the issues on which independent specialist opinion is sought.
3. **[TIMELINE.md](TIMELINE.md)** — the chronology used to test the propositions through time. It separately tracks prescription/plan and actual medication use/response where the record permits.
4. **[complete-record.md](complete-record.md)** — the evidential substrate and source of factual control. Stable `CLIN-` locators permit every material proposition to be checked against the underlying record.

## Governing relationship

> **Argument identifies the issues → expert questions operationalise them → timeline tests their sequence → complete record verifies the facts.**

The essay organises the reader, but it does not override the evidence. If any statement in the essay, questions, or timeline conflicts with `complete-record.md`, the complete record controls.

## Five-proposition map

1. What exactly remained unresolved in 2017?
2. What management architecture was adopted in response?
3. What later evidence materially affected the foot/lower-limb domain?
4. Was that later evidence integrated into a comprehensive re-evaluation, or mainly managed locally within the inherited model?
5. At what point, if any, would reasonable movement-disorders practice have required reopening the original classification?

## Scope

Clinical only: from the presenting history and 2017 referral through longitudinal neurology, podiatry, prescription and medication evidence to the February 2026 PRKN genetic confirmation. GDPR, complaint handling and wider governance are outside this repository except where a clinical document itself forms part of the evidential record.
