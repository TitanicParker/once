# Record Reliability

## Source hierarchy

1. **Contemporaneous objective observation** — neurological examination, Podiatry findings, DaTSCAN, prescriptions.
2. **Contemporaneous patient report** — symptom phenomenology and treatment-state experience, reliable as reported but not self-proving mechanism.
3. **Contemporaneous clinician interpretation** — strong evidence of what clinician believed, not necessarily objective mechanism.
4. **Treatment decision/response** — strong for what was tried and what changed; response does not prove cause.
5. **Later retrospective explanation** — must be checked against earlier sources.
6. **Institutional repetition** — derivative unless independently investigated.
7. **Independent later corroboration** — strong for the matter independently established, e.g. 2026 PRKN genetics.

## Qualified propositions

### “Outside left” = fifth MTPJ
Strong continuity signal, not literal identity at every mention.

### Peripheral neuropathy
A hypothesis/working formulation; not established as a definitive single mechanism for the whole foot syndrome.

### Dystonia
Neurological/dystonic-looking posture is documented; later PRKN letter confirms Parkinsonism and dystonia. It does not prove every historical pain or lesion was dystonic.

### Agreement/understanding
“Good understanding” supports comprehension of the levodopa-delay rationale, not agreement with every causal/service allocation.

## Retrospective-account audit

### 2025 “peripheral neuropathic pain symptomatologies”
Earlier 2017 source language was possibility/“perhaps”/“Not clear.” Later wording may be a reasonable shorthand for a working pain phenotype but should not be treated as proof that a peripheral mechanism was settled in 2017.

### 2025 “idiopathic Parkinson’s disease”
May accurately describe the clinician’s 2025 belief, but is historically selective if presented as a continuous lineage because early probable PARK2/PRKN suspicion is strong and 2026 confirms it.

### 2025 Lyrica “introduced…in part to spare further Sinemet increase”
Later Sinemet-sparing use is genuinely documented. Original initiation provenance points to GP treatment of severe foot/leg pain. No identified contemporaneous source establishes Sinemet-sparing as the original purpose. Classification: **retrospective purpose attribution / possible reconstruction**.

### 2025 Healy as “second opinion”
Accurate in broad terms, but should not imply Healy endorsed every element of Galway’s management policy. His own letter must control the substance.

## State-of-mind boundary

The record supports ordinary compression, selective retrospective framing and possible reconstruction in specific propositions. It does not by itself establish knowing falsity, reckless dishonesty or deliberate deception.
