# SPECIALIST-INPUT

## Purpose

This register identifies specialist and quasi-specialist input relevant to the movement-disorders questions.

It is not a finding that specialist care was inadequate. It records:

- who was involved;
- what expertise or role is evidenced;
- what issue prompted the input;
- what advice or observations were recorded;
- whether follow-up remained available;
- and what later developments may have raised a renewed specialist-input question.

---

## SI04 — Prof Healy's September 2017 specialist opinion

### Role / source

Prof Daniel G. Healy reviewed the patient and wrote to Dr Monaghan on 12 September 2017.

**Complete-record anchors:**  
`CLIN-001734–CLIN-001752`

### Advice and observations recorded

Healy recorded that:

- the patient “probably has Park II”;
- PARK2 should be tested first;
- Parkinsonism probably began in the late 20s or early 30s;
- there had been dramatic motor improvement with procyclidine and rasagiline;
- PARK2 patients may do well for long periods on relatively low dopamine-replacement doses;
- they may become dyskinetic if over-used.

**Complete-record anchors:**  
`CLIN-001742–CLIN-001749`

### Follow-up position

Healy did not arrange routine follow-up but stated that he would be interested in the test results and was happy to review again if required.

**Complete-record anchor:**  
`CLIN-001749`

### Relevance

This is the clearest external movement-disorders-style specialist input in the record and directly bears on genotype, treatment sensitivity and levodopa risk-benefit balance.

**Relevant propositions:** P04, P07, P22

## SI06 — 2018: specialist question potentially renewed by treatment-state evidence

### New evidence

By April 2018 the treating neurologist had recorded:

- rapid return of the outside-left-foot/corn phenomenon during procyclidine withdrawal;
- worsening off procyclidine;
- continued major tension relief on treatment;
- and that levodopa or another dopamine agonist could make the patient “very much better”.

**Complete-record anchors:**  
`CLIN-001890–CLIN-001919`  
`CLIN-001943–CLIN-001956`

### Specialist-input question created

Given:

- suspected PARK2;
- the prior external opinion;
- the clinician's own stated lack of movement-disorders expertise;
- and new foot-specific treatment-state evidence,

did this create a reasonable point for renewed movement-disorders specialist input?

### Relevance

This is not a finding that referral was required. It identifies a plausible re-referral threshold for expert adjudication.

**Relevant propositions:** P04, P05, P07, P22

## SI12 — 2026: genetic confirmation of the earlier PARK2 hypothesis

### Specialist input

The St Vincent's genetic report confirmed two pathogenic Parkin variants and concluded that the patient's parkinsonism and dystonia were due to compound heterozygous Parkin mutations.

**Complete-record anchors:**  
`CLIN-003224–CLIN-003229`

### Relevance

This confirms the disease mechanism after the period under review.

It does not establish that clinicians were required to know the diagnosis earlier.

It does, however, confirm that the 2017 PARK2 hypothesis was clinically substantive rather than speculative noise.

**Relevant propositions:** P04, P22

## Core specialist-input question

The documentary record establishes:

1. an unusually young and atypical Parkinsonian presentation;
2. explicit suspicion of PARK2/PRKN;
3. treating-clinician acknowledgement of lack of movement-disorders expertise;
4. external specialist review by Prof Healy;
5. ongoing availability of further review;
6. repeated later treatment-complexity triggers.

The unresolved expert question is:

**At what point, if any, did movement-disorders specialist assessment or continuing co-management become clinically indicated rather than merely optional?**

That question should be answered independently and not presumed by this register.
