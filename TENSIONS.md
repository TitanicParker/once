# TENSIONS

## Purpose

This register identifies documentary tensions that arise when the propositions, facts, decision points, specialist input, and longitudinal treatment history are read together.

A tension is not necessarily a contradiction.

A tension exists where two or more propositions can coexist, but their coexistence creates a clinical or evidential question requiring explanation, weighting, or model revision.

The purpose of this file is therefore to identify **what required adjudication**, not to assume which side is correct.

- dystonic-looking foot-drop-like posture;

**Complete-record anchors:**

# Overarching tension

The strongest longitudinal tension in the record is not:

**“Was the foot pain neurological or structural?”**

It is:

**How did a management framework built under early uncertainty respond when accumulating patient-specific evidence changed the balance between diagnosis, treatment benefit, treatment risk, and lower-limb medication-state responsiveness?**

The record supports the coexistence of:

- real structural foot pathology;
- real neurological lower-limb abnormalities;
- real treatment benefit;
- real treatment toxicity;
- real neuropathic-type pain;
- real dopaminergic OFF phenomena;
- genuine uncertainty at different stages.

The expert task is to determine whether these were progressively re-synthesised into an updated model, or whether important evidence remained locally acknowledged without equivalent longitudinal influence.
