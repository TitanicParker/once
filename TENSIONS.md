# Tensions

A tension is not a finding of fault. It is a pair or group of facts that cannot be collapsed without expert interpretation.

## T-01 — GP integrated presentation ↔ Neurology component allocation
GP referred gait, feet, tone, toe clawing, initiation and work failure as one neurological problem. Neurology documented severe Parkinsonism but routed local feet to Podiatry while recording causal relation “Not clear.”

## T-02 — “Not clear” ↔ definite service destination
Causal relationship remained open; Podiatry destination became definite. The service pathway was resolved more clearly than the clinical relationship.

## T-03 — Genuine structural disease ↔ neurological lower-limb disease
Repeated fifth-MTPJ lesions, deformity and mechanical benefit are real. So are dystonic-looking posture, rigidity, bradykinesia, later Parkinsonian foot throw, OFF states and dyskinesia.

## T-04 — Patient early separation ↔ later treatment-state integration
Patient initially distinguished foot pain from leg tension and reported reduced foot pain with less standing. Later he repeatedly supplied medication-state observations involving feet/gait.

## T-05 — Focal left fifth-MTPJ signal ↔ broader later pain syndromes
A persistent focal left-lateral lesion/pain signal exists, but later bilateral burning, coronal pain and morning soreness are materially broader.

## T-06 — Galway “hold back” ↔ Healy low-dose replacement model
Galway asked whether to hold back levodopa. Healy supported probable PARK2 and warned against over-use but described long-term management with relatively low-dose dopamine replacement. Neither statement proves immediate levodopa was required.

## T-07 — Healy probable PARK2 ↔ later idiopathic framing
Early specialist opinion and 2018 Neurology language keep PRKN/PARK2 live. The 2025 complaint response states a belief/understanding of idiopathic Parkinson’s without an identified documentary bridge explaining the transition.

## T-08 — Procyclidine withdrawal evidence ↔ unchanged global architecture
Neurological drug withdrawal altered feet/gait/tension. Local response was to re-escalate drug; no documented global reopening of the feet/Podiatry partition is identified.

## T-09 — Treatment caution ↔ symptom burden
Dyskinesia/ICD/chorea risks were real and patient-specific. So were severe disability, pain and OFF phenomena. The case is a risk-benefit problem, not a one-directional undertreatment story.

## T-10 — 2020 Parkinsonian foot finding ↔ persistent pain partition
A neurological foot movement was explicitly identified. This did not automatically establish that focal or burning pain had the same mechanism.

## T-11 — 2021 major treatment substitution ↔ absent foot outcome map
Kemadrin was replaced with Sinemet, but no structured foot-by-dose outcome map is visible despite earlier treatment-state sensitivity.

## T-12 — 2022 morning warm-up ↔ no proven medication-onset interpretation
The pattern is compatible with coverage issues but also mechanical warming, pain adaptation or mixed causes.

## T-13 — 2023 neuropathic crisis ↔ 2023 OFF/undertreatment evidence
Severe bilateral burning and pregabalin benefit support a pain pathway; OFF shuffling/leg pain, Stalevo-enabled function and evening lack-of-levodopa analysis support neurological modulation.

## T-14 — Ten-day medication cessation report ↔ other dopaminergic response evidence
The patient's no-difference report is genuine counterevidence and prevents an unbroken dopaminergic-dependence narrative.

## T-15 — Lyrica pain relief ↔ persistent toe curling
Pain and posture are partly dissociable, requiring separate outcomes.

## T-16 — GP initiation of Lyrica ↔ 2025 Sinemet-sparing origin claim
Contemporaneous record says GP initiated pregabalin for severe pain. Later Neurology says it was introduced partly to spare further Sinemet. Later balancing is documented; original purpose attribution is not.

## T-17 — Local management change ↔ global reassessment
Treatment changed repeatedly: procyclidine, agonists, Sinemet, Stalevo, pregabalin, CR. No comparably explicit note is identified that reconstructs the original 2017 mixed classification in light of the whole later sequence.

## T-18 — Complaint-triggered review ↔ retrospective defence
The complaint triggered a response, but the response quotes and defends the 2017 levodopa-sparing strategy rather than expressly abandoning it.

## T-19 — CR profound relief ↔ mechanism remains mixed
CR relief is strong therapeutic evidence. The treating explanation still frames Parkinson’s as magnifying pain “of whatever cause.” Response does not prove the generator.

## T-20 — PRKN confirmation ↔ no back-projection
2026 validates the genetic branch and dystonia diagnosis but cannot itself prove the earlier foot pain mechanism or earlier treatment standard.
