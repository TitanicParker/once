# Timeline — Hinge Events

## 2017

- **10 Apr — original foot presentation.** Bilateral foot pain, abnormal gait, marked mallet toes, onychogryphosis, callus/pressure areas; Podiatry planned. **CLIN-000018–000039.**
- **8–29 Jun — problem becomes neurological.** GP links gait, feet and “muscle tension”; initiation difficulty, marked leg tone, brisk reflexes, deteriorating dexterity and work failure. **CLIN-000064–000085; CLIN-000190–000225; CLIN-000847–000884.**
- **6 Jul — GP re-contextualises Podiatry “as Parkinson’s.”** **CLIN-000899–000912.**
- **3–11 Jul — hospital neurological phenotype.** Markedly increased tone, striking gait, foot-drop-like dystonic posture “NOT FOOT DROP,” no arm swing, rigidity, bradykinesia, pes planus, hammertoes. **CLIN-001085–001120.**
- **Jul — causal fork.** Corns/calluses/burning may be separate or peripheral neuropathy; relationship “Not clear.” Patient historically distinguishes foot pain from leg tension. **CLIN-001142–001149; CLIN-000965–000976.**
- **Jul — management fork.** Great caution with levodopa/dopamine agonists because exceptionally young onset, rapid dyskinesia and ICD risk; likely avoid “as long as possible”; procyclidine/rasagiline/clonazepam alternatives; Podiatry requested. **CLIN-001166–001229.**
- **13 Jul — DaTSCAN.** Very young bilateral Parkinsonism, gross bradykinesia, “walk is almost dystonically unusual,” autosomal-recessive Parkinsonism suspected. **CLIN-001389–001408.**
- **2017 Healy request.** Galway asks genetic advice and whether holding back levodopa should change: “unless you'd advise otherwise?” **CLIN-001720–001730.**
- **12 Sep — Healy.** “Probably has Park II”; recommends PARK2 testing; low-dose dopamine replacement often manageable long term, but over-use may produce dyskinesia. **CLIN-001741–001760.**
- **Sep 2017 onward — recurrent left fifth-MTPJ pathology.** First exact site documented 14 Sep; repeated through Mar 2019. **CLIN-002860–002988.**
- **Oct — procyclidine natural experiment.** Major global motor/function improvement; withdrawal: tension builds, feet restless, walk falls apart; eyesight improves off. **CLIN-001770–001813.**

## 2018–2019

- **2018 — PARK2 remains live.** Neurology states specific PARK2/PRKN is what “we/Prof Healy suspect is the cause,” while considering genetics unlikely to change current management. **CLIN-001950–001957.**
- **7 Dec 2018 — focal convergence.** “Pain under the left side of my left foot”; objective callus at left fifth MTPJ; targeted fifth-MTPJ offloading. **CLIN-002965–002973.**
- **2019 — dopamine agonist toxicity/risk becomes patient-specific.** Tightrope between stiffness/discomfort and impulsivity/compulsions/chorea-dyskinesia. Levodopa to be deferred “as long as is humanly possible.” **CLIN-002147–002166.**
- **2019 GP Podiatry re-referral.** Re-links foot problems to increased tone, abnormal Parkinsonian gait and “clawing feet” with pain. **CLIN-003091–003101.**

## 2020–2021

- **2020 — neurological foot movement.** Foot-throw appearance is classified “actually parkinsonian” because strength is normal; marked sensitivity/excess movement also recorded. **CLIN-002282–002290.**
- **Sep/Oct 2021 — first levodopa.** Sinemet 62.5 mg BD for undertreatment; Kemadrin removed for ocular-pressure concerns; Sinemet increased to QID. **CLIN-002330–002354.**
- **2021 — modified dopamine-sparing principle persists.** Neurology does not want Sinemet rising significantly; aims to keep it “as absolutely minimal as possible.” **CLIN-002351–002354.**
- **2021 measurement gap.** No structured foot-by-dose map is identified for onset, wearing-off, overnight gap, morning steps, focal lateral-left pain, clawing/curling or loading.

## 2022–2024

- **2022 — morning foot phenotype.** Feet take 20–25 minutes to “warm up,” are sore, with “coronal pain” around edges; Sinemet timing varies with functional demands. **CLIN-002378–002386.**
- **Mar 2023 — severe broad pain crisis.** GP reports severe bilateral burning feet/lower legs, “death wish”; pregabalin began at 25 mg BD and had been escalated through doctors, only just controlling symptoms. **CLIN-002637–002641.**
- **Mar 2023 — contrary report.** Parkinson nurse records patient stopped Parkinson medications for ten days and perceived no Parkinsonian difference. **CLIN-002681–002684.**
- **2023 — OFF/undertreatment evidence.** Severe foot pain plus shuffling/reduced mobility, worse mornings/later if Sinemet not taken; nurse judges leg pain/shuffling OFF-related; Stalevo commenced. **CLIN-002574–002582.**
- **2023 — evening OFF analysis.** Typical OFF protective stance/lack movement/short steps; Stalevo facilitates darts; insufficient levodopa considered; evening levodopa/CR options discussed. **CLIN-002449–002474.**
- **2023 — Lyrica provenance.** Nurse explicitly says patient “was commenced on Lyrica by his G.p.” **CLIN-002681–002684.**
- **2024 — pain/posture dissociation.** Lyrica removes much foot pain, especially right, while toe curling persists. **CLIN-002698–002710.**

## 2025–2026

- **2025 — Lyrica self-reduction and severe morning feet.** Maximum 600 mg/day reduced by patient to 50 mg TDS; Neurology states it had not advised reduction. Feet mainly left; “murder in the feet,” first 150 footsteps “torture”; relief once Parkinson medication hits system. **CLIN-002767–002800.**
- **2025 — explicit balancing.** More Sinemet expected to relieve some pain but worsen dyskinesia; increasing Lyrica considered to avoid Parkinson-medication increase; nighttime Sinemet CR then prescribed around overnight gap. **CLIN-002787–002800.**
- **25 Mar 2025 — complaint response.** Historical levodopa-sparing policy expressly defended; Sinemet benefit framed as reducing pain experience “of whatever cause”; Lyrica retrospectively said to have been introduced partly to spare further Sinemet; CR said to provide “profound relief currently.” **CLIN-003239–003270.**
- **4 Feb 2026 — genetics.** Two pathogenic Parkin variants confirmed; Parkinsonism and dystonia attributed to compound heterozygous PRKN mutations. **CLIN-003220–003228.**
