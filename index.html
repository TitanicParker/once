When does an unresolved clinical partition have to be reopened?

A longitudinal documentary analysis of structural foot pathology, pain, Parkinsonism, dystonia, medication state, treatment risk and the management architecture that connected — and separated — them.

Central issue

The strongest case is longitudinal, not binary.

The record does not support a simple claim that every foot symptom was Parkinsonian, that Podiatry was inappropriate, or that levodopa necessarily should have begun in July 2017. Structural disease was real. Neurological disease was real. Their relationship was expressly left unresolved.

An expressly unresolved mixed structural–neurological presentation was converted into workable parallel management pathways. The central question is whether, and when, accumulating evidence required the original relationship and management architecture to be explicitly reopened rather than repeatedly updated only at component level.

Fact discipline

Mixed pathology stays mixed

Structural deformity, focal fifth-MTPJ pathology, pain, dystonia/posture, gait, neuropathic hypotheses and medication-state effects are not collapsed into a single variable.

Causal discipline

Response is not mechanism

Procyclidine, pregabalin, Stalevo and Sinemet responses can show treatment sensitivity without proving the original generator of pain or structural lesions.

Hindsight discipline

PRKN is not back-projected

The 2026 genetic result validates the early PARK2 branch, but it cannot be treated as knowledge available to clinicians in 2017.

Longitudinal hinges

The points at which the architecture could be tested

April–July 2017Bilateral foot pain, structural pathology, severe young-onset Parkinsonism and dystonic-looking gait converge. Neurology records the relationship as “Not clear.”
September 2017Healy considers probable PARK2 and describes cautious low-dose dopamine replacement with dyskinesia risk if over-used.
October 2017Procyclidine withdrawal produces deterioration involving tension, feet and gait: an early treatment-state signal inside the disputed lower-limb domain.
2017–2019Repeated left fifth-MTPJ callus/HD establishes a genuine persistent focal structural signal; Dec 2018 directly links left-sided pain, objective lesion and targeted offloading.
2020Neurology classifies the foot-throw phenomenon as actually Parkinsonian, strengthening the neurological component without resolving pain mechanism.
2021Sinemet begins. Literal levodopa deferral ends, but the higher-order dopamine-sparing policy remains explicit. The record does not show structured foot-by-dose outcome measurement.
2022–2024Morning foot warm-up, OFF/undertreatment evidence, high-dose pregabalin, bilateral burning pain and persistent toe curling reveal multiple overlapping phenotypes.
2025Overnight gap, first-step pain and relief after Parkinson medication lead to targeted Sinemet CR; later response records “profound relief.”
2025 complaintThe dispute is reopened, but the response largely defends the historical management logic and retains pain “of whatever cause” as compatible with levodopa benefit.
2026Two pathogenic PRKN variants confirm Parkin-related Parkinsonism/dystonia, validating the early genetic branch without retrospectively proving the foot-pain mechanism.
Adversarial balance

Two serious readings of the same record

Strongest benign explanation

  • Structural foot disease was genuine and Podiatry was appropriate.
  • The patient himself initially distinguished mechanical foot pain from leg tension.
  • Treatment toxicity was real: ocular effects, chorea, dyskinesia and impulse-control concerns.
  • Pregabalin materially helped pain while toe curling persisted, supporting a mixed model.
  • OFF states were recognised and treated as they became clearer.
  • CR was introduced once the overnight temporal pattern became explicit.

Strongest path-dependent interpretation

  • A provisional 2017 solution to uncertainty became a durable organising architecture.
  • Cross-domain evidence repeatedly generated local treatment changes without a visible global reconstruction.
  • The architecture could preserve separate primary pain while explaining neurological drug effects as modulation.
  • The 2025 complaint response reaches back to the original levodopa-sparing rationale rather than documenting abandonment of the old frame.
  • The expert question is what evidence should have required the original partition itself to return to an open state.

Management plan

Medication changes are not the same thing as a changed explanatory architecture.

Literal levodopa deferral ended in 2021. The more difficult longitudinal question is whether the higher-order policy — dopamine minimisation because of young-age toxicity, use of alternative symptom routes where possible, and preservation of a potentially separate structural/peripheral pain generator — remained functionally alive after individual treatments changed.

The repository therefore distinguishes local management change, global reassessment, continuation of prior policy, and retrospective defence of prior policy.
